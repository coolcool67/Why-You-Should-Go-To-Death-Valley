Desert trivia

Amazing Death Valley facts

A landscape of record-setting extremes and surprising details.

🏜️ 3.4 million

Acres of parkland

It’s the largest national park in the contiguous United States.

⬇️ −282 ft

North America’s low point

Badwater Basin lies below sea level.

⛰️ 11,049 ft

Telescope Peak

The park’s highest point rises above the desert basin.

🌡️ 134°F

Record temperature

The National Park Service reports this Furnace Creek reading from July 1913.

📅 1994

National park since

Death Valley was designated a national park in 1994.

Explore smart

Enjoy Death Valley safely—with a plan.

Many people visit safely, but extreme heat and remote conditions can be dangerous. Prepare before you go, follow park guidance, and change your plans if conditions become unsafe.

  • Check the weather, road conditions, and park alerts before leaving.
  • Carry plenty of water and avoid strenuous activity in extreme heat.
  • Tell someone your plans, and don’t rely on cell service.
  • Turn back if you or anyone in your group feels unwell.
